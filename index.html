To My Dearest Babe,

One whole year of us, and forever to go...

Happy 1st Anniversary, Linda.

I sat down trying to find the right words to capture what these past 365 days have meant to me, and honestly, my eyes started tearing up because no words will ever feel big enough for what I feel for you. A whole year ago today, our story officially began—and looking back now, I realize that saying "yes" to us was the single greatest blessing of my life.

To the world, your name is Linda. Buh to me, you will always be my Babe—my safe place, my peace, my loudest cheerleader, and the person my heart instinctively searches for in every room. Every single time I call you "Babe," it isn't just a pet name; it is a reminder that you are mine, and I am completely, unconditionally yours.

Before you walked into my life, I didn't know love could feel this gentle yet this powerful. You didn't just become my girlfriend; you became my home. In a world that can be so loud, stressful, and unpredictable, your voice is the one thing that calms my storm. Your laugh is my favorite sound on this earth, and seeing you smile makes every hard day worth it.

"If I had to live this past year all over again, I wouldn't change a single second—because every moment led me deeper in love with you."

Thank you, Babe, for loving me on my best days and holding me closer on my hardest ones. Thank you for your patience, your kindness, your warmth, and the way you care so deeply about us. You have seen parts of me no one else sees, and you loved me anyway. You have made me a better man, a happier soul, and someone who looks forward to tomorrow simply because you are in it.

One year down, Linda, and my heart still skips a beat whenever I look at you. This first year was just the introduction to the greatest love story I could ever ask for. I want every anniversary after this one. I want the quiet mornings, the late-night talks, the dreams we haven't built yet, and growing old with my hand in yours.

I love you more than words on a screen could ever hold, more than yesterday, and less than I will tomorrow. Happy one year, my love.

Forever & Always Yours,

Your Man ❤️
